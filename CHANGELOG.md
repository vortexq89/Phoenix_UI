### Chat and Vault (step 8b)
- Chat (plan 5.10, mockup 06): rebuilt on Blizzard's own chat frames with no AddMessage hook, no FCF_* hook and no reparenting. Phoenix draws a panel behind each window (Phoenix, Glass or Blizzard look, colour, opacity, padding, border) and sets the font, size, outline and shadow. Lines are decorated as they are shown, through the message-event filter: timestamps (click one to copy the line), short channel names ([2. T] or [2]), clickable links that open the copy box, emoji with suggestions while you type (click or Tab), keyword alerts (highlight colour, sound, icon flash, ignore my own lines), role icons, hide repeated lines, and a one-line battleground join/leave summary you can click for the names.
- Chat rules: window 1 is placed only by Edit Mode (`/pui chat editmode`), and window 3 (voice transcription) is never touched. A line with any secret argument is passed back unchanged, so it looks exactly like stock Blizzard. Alerts, history and spam checks run once per line even when the line shows in several windows. Idle fade runs on the shared Ticker and wakes on a new line, on hover or while typing.
- Chat history (`/pui chat history`): filter by text, channel, player and date (today, 7 days, 30 days, all), copy the results, and keep lines across reloads (lines and days kept are settings). Lines the game kept secret are never stored.
- Smart whisper windows (off by default): one window with a fixed-width tab per person (tabs shrink evenly when they overflow), unread counts and a reply box. They collect quietly or pop up, never in combat. Sending is refused during chat lockdown, with a note. Channel bar (off by default): plain buttons above or below window 1.
- Announcement router (`PUI.Announce`, QoL page, plan 1.O): the one place modules send chat announcements through. It is off at first install and sends only to you by default (other channels: group, party, raid, instance, say, yell, guild). A grid sets each topic per content type, and everything starts unticked. Repeats are skipped, and during lockdown or for secret text a line is shown only to you.
- Vault (plan 5.11, mockup 11): category view by default with a rules engine. You write your own categories as `Name|rule` (fields type, subtype, ilvl, quality, name, id, bound, expansion, slot, count, set, keystone; and/or/not, brackets, `field:value`), and they are checked before the built-in Pinned, New, Keystone shelf, Equipment sets, Equipment, Consumables, Reagents, Quest, Other and Junk categories. You can show or collapse each category. Pin mode and junk mode let you click items to add them without using the item. Manual "itemID=Category" assignments are supported. The views are category, grid, compact and list (names and counts), and the empty-slot fold, slot size, spacing and columns are settings.
- Vault junk (owner): auto-sell is OFF at first install. The Sell junk button sells greys through the game's sell-all and items you marked one by one, at most 12 a visit so they stay in buyback. Pinned items are never junk, and Flow's sell-junk stands down while Vault is on.
- Undo the last sort: Phoenix snapshots every slot before sorting, then puts items back one swap at a time on a Ticker entry. This only runs out of combat and only on unlocked slots. Stacks the sort merged are reported and left where they are.
- Search: plain words use the game's search, and text with an operator (`ilvl>650`, `set:PvP`) uses the rules engine.
- Quality borders use ColorManager, so your colour overrides apply.
- Bank window at the banker has Character, Warband, Reagents, Guild bank (read-only, current tab) and Alts tabs. The live tabs use the game's secure slot buttons, and the rest show a read-only picture from your last visit. You can deposit reagents and sort. Blizzard's bank window is faded and click-through while it shows, and the FrameLedger restores it.
- Alts and gold: alts' bag and bank items are remembered, with item counts across characters. Gold history is recorded once a day per character into `PUI.Wallet` (the owner; Data Bars read it), with a total and the last week on the gold line's tooltip. Currencies show in the footer. `/pui vault gold wipe` asks before it forgets anything. Equipment sets link to Loadouts.
- Conflict notices: Chat (Prat-3.0, Chattynator, Chatter, Glass, WIM, BasicChatMods) and Bags (Baganator, Bagnon, AdiBags, ArkInventory, BetterBags, Combuctor, LiteBag). You pick which one keeps the job.
- Live previews for Chat (sample lines through the real text helpers) and Vault (sample items grouped by the real rules engine with your rules).
- Settings carry over: the old short-channel toggle and the old bag view become the new settings, and none are reset.
- Tests: 13 new offline cases and 5 rewritten (244 in total, retail and Forever); harness stubs for chat frames, new-item, equipment-set, bank and keystone APIs.
