# Bundled sounds

Every sound here is dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credit is not required by CC0; it is given anyway.
They reached us through BigWigs' media folder, whose licence notes list the original authors below; the
files are unchanged Ogg Vorbis conversions.

| File | Phoenix name | Author | Original |
|---|---|---|---|
| AlertOnYou.ogg | Phoenix: Alert (on you) | unfa | https://freesound.org/people/unfa/sounds/215416/ |
| AlertUnderYou.ogg | Phoenix: Alert (under you) | unfa | https://freesound.org/people/unfa/sounds/240875/ |
| Countdown/Jim/1–10.ogg | Phoenix: Countdown voice "Jim" | tekgnosis | https://freesound.org/people/tekgnosis/sounds/151268/ (cut into numbers) |
| Countdown/David/1–10.ogg | Phoenix: Countdown voice "David" | davidbain | https://freesound.org/people/davidbain/sounds/259705/ (cut into numbers) |

The two alerts are registered with LibSharedMedia (type "sound"); the countdown voices are used by the
Auras & Cooldowns tracker and the sound pickers.
